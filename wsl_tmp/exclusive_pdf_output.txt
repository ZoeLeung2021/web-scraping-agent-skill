LOUIS LATOURCHATEAU CORTON
GRANCEY
Tasting Notes
Aromas: Cassis, saffron and earthiness.
Sight: A purple hue this Grand Cru exhales.
Taste: In the mouth the tannins envelop a lovely cherry fruitiness and are
powerful yet silky.
Food Pairing
Wild Boar - Roasted beef - roasted quail - duck "à l'orange" - mature cheeses.
